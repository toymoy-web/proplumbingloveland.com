A Town That Grew More Than Fivefold

What Rapid Growth Means For Plumbing Calls

The census counted 1,622 people in Milliken in 1990, 3,015 in 2000, 5,614 in 2010, and 8,386 in 2020. That is more than five times the population in thirty years, and most of the growth is subdivision housing built since the early 2000s. In practice that means a lot of homes of the same age, with the same water heaters, the same fixtures, and the same builder-grade parts reaching the end of their warranties around the same time.

Old Town Milliken is older than most people expect. The post office opened on November 10, 1909, and the Daniels School was built in 1911. Old Town, centered near South Irene Avenue where the town's early school house stood, is the part of Milliken most likely to still have galvanized supply lines, clay or cast iron sewer laterals, and original water services.

Two Towns, One School District

Milliken And Johnstown Along Highway 60

The Johnstown and Milliken school districts merged in 1961 into Weld RE-5J, and the two towns still share it. For our dispatch the closeness shows up on the map, since Milliken sits only about four miles east of Johnstown on the same Highway 60 route.

From Loveland the usual path is US-34 to I-25 and then east on Highway 60 through Johnstown, roughly 16 miles. Milliken is in Weld County, so permits and inspections follow the Town of Milliken and Weld County rather than Loveland's city rules, and our technicians handle that on permitted work.

Same-Age Homes, Same-Age Problems

Why Whole Streets Call In Waves

When a subdivision is built in a short window, its water heaters, fixtures, and supply parts age together. A tank installed in 2008 reaches the end of its typical life around the same time as the tank next door, and builder-grade faucets and toilet fill valves tend to wear out in the same few years. We often get several calls from one street within a month or two of each other.

That pattern is useful to know before you call. If a neighbor just replaced a water heater or a failing shutoff valve, it is worth having yours looked at while it still works. A planned replacement on a Tuesday afternoon is cheaper and calmer than an emergency replacement at midnight.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Milliken

  • Turn the main water valve fully off, then check that no fixture is still running.
  • If water is reaching a light fixture, an outlet, or the panel, stay out of that room.
  • Pull rugs and low furniture out of the wet area if you can do it safely.
