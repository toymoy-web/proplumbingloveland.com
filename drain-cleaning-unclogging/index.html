Common Clogs

What's Usually Behind It

Kitchen Sink Clog

Grease, food scraps, and coffee grounds build up inside the trap and line.

Bathroom Drain Clog

Hair and soap scum are the most common cause in tubs and sinks.

Main Line Backup

Every fixture draining slowly at once usually points here, not one fixture.

Tree Root Intrusion

Roots enter older clay or cast iron lines through cracks and joints.

If It's An Emergency

Sewage Backup Or Multiple Drains Backed Up

A single slow drain can usually wait for a scheduled visit, but multiple fixtures backing up at once, or any sign of sewage coming up through a floor drain or tub, is treated as an emergency and dispatched immediately. Our line is answered 24/7 for exactly this kind of call, and a technician brings both cabling and jetting equipment so the right tool is already on the truck.

Multiple fixtures backing up together is the detail that actually matters when deciding how urgent a call is. A single slow kitchen sink is almost always local to that fixture's trap or branch line. When a toilet, tub, and another sink all start draining slowly around the same time, the blockage is downstream of all of them, in the main line, which means it will keep getting worse until it's cleared rather than resolving on its own. That distinction is exactly what we ask about on the phone before dispatching, since it changes both the urgency and which equipment the technician brings.

How We Clear It

Snaking, Jetting, Or A Camera First

Most single-fixture clogs come out with a drain snake in one visit. Grease buildup, recurring clogs, or a line that's clogged again within a few months usually calls for hydro jetting instead, since it scours the entire pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the blockage. For a main line or a clog that keeps coming back, a camera inspection shows exactly what's going on, root intrusion, a bellied pipe, or a collapsed section, before we recommend a fix.

Loveland's older neighborhoods near downtown are more likely to carry clay or cast iron sewer lines from the mid-1900s, and both materials are far more root-friendly than the PVC used in newer construction. If a camera inspection turns up roots in a clay line, cabling alone tends to be a temporary fix since it clears a path through the roots without removing them, and the same spot often clogs again within a year. Jetting combined with a root-cutting head does a more complete job on that kind of line.

Older Drain Lines

Clogs In Historic Downtown And West Central Loveland

Homes in Historic Downtown Loveland and West Central Loveland predate modern PVC drains, and many still have cast iron or clay lines with rough interior walls that catch grease and roots. Mature trees along the older streets near Lake Loveland make root intrusion a regular cause of repeat clogs. Newer neighborhoods like Centerra more often see clogs from grease, wipes, and kitchen waste.

Reading The Symptoms

What The Pattern Of Backups Is Telling You

Where the water shows up matters. A washer that backs up into the tub points to the main line. A kitchen sink that fills the laundry sink points to a blockage downstream of both. A toilet that gurgles when another fixture drains means air is trapped behind a partial clog.

One Slow Drain

Usually a local clog in the trap or the branch line.

Two Or More Drains

The shared branch or the main line is the likely problem.

Basement Floor Drain Rising

Treat it as a main line backup and hold off on water upstairs.

Gurgling Toilet

Air pulled past a partial blockage, often a sign it is getting worse.

When The Snake Stops Short

A cable that stalls at one depth, or one that cannot get past a bend, means the clog sits farther along or the pipe is damaged. A camera shows which. If roots or grease built up the blockage, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the middle.

Sewer Smell Or Standing Tub Water

A sewer odor in one bathroom can come from a dry trap, a worn seal, or a vent problem rather than a clog. Water that sits in a tub long after use points the other way and should be checked soon. Describe the smell and when it appears. If the line itself looks damaged, the sewer line repair page picks up from there.
