Why This Matters Here

Front Range Water And Tankless Units

Loveland draws from Front Range surface water with a meaningful mineral load, and a tankless heater has almost no tolerance for that mineral load compared to a tank. A tank spreads sediment across a large reservoir. A tankless unit forces water through a narrow heat exchanger every time a tap opens, so scale collects on that one small surface fast. Left alone, scale buildup on the exchanger is the single biggest cause of early tankless failure and reduced hot water output in this area, well before the unit's rated lifespan runs out.

Signs You Need Service

Common Tankless Complaints

Lukewarm, Not Hot

Water reaches the tap warm instead of hot, usually a scale-restricted heat exchanger.

Delayed Hot Water

A longer-than-normal wait before hot water arrives at a fixture.

Unit Cycling Or Clicking

Short-cycling or an audible clicking sound as the burner repeatedly fires and cuts out.

Error Code On Display

A flashing code, usually tied to flow, ignition, or temperature sensors.

What's Actually Wrong

Flush Issue Or Failing Unit?

A surprising number of the calls we get about a "broken" tankless unit turn out to be a maintenance issue, not a failed appliance. Reduced flow, inconsistent temperature, and even some error codes are what a scaled-up heat exchanger looks like from the homeowner's side, and a flush and descale service brings the unit back to full output in a single visit. We check flow rate, inspect the exchanger through the service port, and test the igniter and venting before recommending anything beyond that, because a homeowner calling about tepid water rarely actually needs a whole new unit.

Service Options

Flush, Repair, Or Replace

When a flush alone doesn't resolve the problem, the next step depends on what's actually failed. A cracked heat exchanger, a failed gas valve, or a burned-out control board are repairable on most major brands, provided parts are available. A unit past 15-20 years old, or one that's failed more than once in the last two years, usually costs less to replace outright than to keep repairing, and we'll say so plainly rather than stacking repair visits on an aging unit.

Where Tankless Units Show Up

Tankless Systems From Centerra To The Boyd Lake Area

Tankless units turn up most often in newer builds, including The Lakes at Centerra and the neighborhoods around Boyd Lake State Park, where a wall-mounted unit in a garage or utility room is the norm. Front Range hard water scales the heat exchanger in all of them, so annual flushing matters just as much in a 2020 Centerra home as it does in a retrofit inside an older house near downtown.

Service History And Codes

Error Codes, Scale, And Units Other Companies Turned Away

A common complaint is hearing from another company that they replace tankless units but do not service them. This page exists for the opposite call. If you have a tankless unit that needs a flush, a descale, or a diagnosis, start the conversation with the brand and model.

Reading An Error Code

The codes callers mention most involve ignition, flame sensing, or flow. A burnt sensor wire, a scaled heat exchanger, or a blocked inlet filter can each trigger one. Photograph the display, note the beeping pattern, and share the unit age. Do not open the cabinet on a gas unit.

Descale And Flush Questions

What a descale costs depends on the unit, how easy it is to reach, and how much scale has built up. Mineral-rich Front Range water shortens the interval, as covered above. Ask what the visit includes, such as isolation valves, the pump, and cleaning of the inlet screen. A unit with no isolation valves may need them added first.

Boilers And Combination Setups

Some homes pair a tankless unit with an old boiler. If the plan includes removing an old boiler and tank, say so, since that changes the scope. A tired unit that is not worth servicing moves to the replacement page.
