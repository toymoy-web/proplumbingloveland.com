Match The Symptom

Where To Start When You Only Know What You See

Water on the floor with no obvious source starts with leak detection, and a spray you cannot stop starts with burst pipe repair. A sink or tub that will not drain belongs with drain cleaning, and a line that keeps clogging may need jetting. No hot water starts with water heater repair. A high water bill with no visible leak starts with the meter test on the leak detection page.

Rentals and larger buildings usually belong with commercial plumbing. A basement taking on water goes to sump pump repair. If none of these fits, describe the problem and the room, and the dispatcher picks the right page and technician.

What To Do Before You Call

  • Close the main water valve if water is spraying or pooling and you can reach it safely.
  • Stay out of standing water near outlets, panels, and appliances.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility from outside.
  • Have your address and a short description of the problem ready.
What To Say On The Call

Four Details That Speed Everything Up

Say where the water or the problem is, whether you can shut it off, what type of property it is, and whether anyone tried a repair first. Have photos on your phone to describe what you see. Those details let the dispatcher send the right tools the first time.

How We Price Every Job

One Standard Across All Services

Every service listed here follows the same pricing approach: a flat price before any work begins, confirmed after we've actually diagnosed the problem rather than guessing over the phone. This applies whether it's a same-day faucet repair or a full sewer line replacement, and it applies the same way whether you're calling from Loveland itself or one of the nearby towns we serve.
