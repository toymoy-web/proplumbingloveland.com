The Community

What Mariana Butte Is, And What We Do Not Claim

According to its course listing, Mariana Butte Golf Course is a public 18-hole course designed by Dick Phelps that opened in 1992. Homes around it carry the Mariana Butte name in local listings, on streets such as Clubhouse Drive and Mariana Pointe Drive. Loveland does not publish a formal boundary for the community, so we describe it by those anchor streets and the course.

The homes were not all built at once. Listings describe established houses alongside newer construction, including a section called The Enclave at Mariana Butte. We will not guess your home's age. If you know the build year or the water heater's age, tell the dispatcher and the technician plans around it.

Practical Guidance

Homes With Yards, Sprinklers And Cold Winters

Many west side homes have lawns, which usually means an irrigation system. A sprinkler line tied into the home's water supply normally has a backflow assembly, and those assemblies need periodic testing. If yours has not been checked lately, see our backflow testing page.

Winter is the other seasonal job. Front Range cold snaps can freeze hose bibs and exposed sprinkler fittings. Drain outdoor lines before the first hard freeze and learn where your main shutoff is. Our winterizing guide walks through the steps.

The most useful number you can know is your water heater's age. A leak from the tank body usually means replacement, while a leak from a fitting or valve can often be repaired. Tankless units need periodic flushing to clear mineral scale. Whether you choose repair, replacement or a tankless unit, we lay out the options after we see the unit.

A sudden jump in your water bill, a meter that keeps moving with every fixture off, or a soggy patch in the lawn can point to a hidden leak. Irrigation lines are worth checking, since they run underground and can leak for weeks unseen. Our leak detection page explains how we locate the source before anything gets dug up.

Plumbing work in Colorado follows the state plumbing code, and some jobs, such as a water heater replacement, can require a permit. We tell you what applies before work starts.

Before We Arrive: Leak, Burst Pipe Or Frozen Line

  • Close the main water valve. Know its location before winter, not during an emergency.
  • Keep clear of any wet area near outlets, switches or the electrical panel.
  • For a frozen line, leave faucets slightly open and do not use a flame to thaw it.
  • Call our 24/7 emergency line with your street and a short description.
