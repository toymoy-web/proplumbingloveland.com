Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-16.

Information We Collect

When you contact us by phone or email, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, address, and a description of the service you need. Our website also uses standard analytics tools that collect general usage data, such as pages visited and general geographic region, and phone calls to our tracked numbers may be recorded or analyzed for quality and marketing purposes.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to schedule and complete service calls, respond to inquiries, and maintain accurate service records. Website analytics data helps us understand which pages are useful to visitors and improve our site accordingly. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Google Maps to display location information and may use third-party analytics services. These providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Our call tracking numbers are provided through a third-party call tracking service used to measure marketing effectiveness.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics purposes. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended if you do so.

Colorado Privacy Act

Colorado residents have certain rights under the Colorado Privacy Act regarding personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information you provide to us, though no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
