Loveland, CO Service Area

Based in Loveland, CO, we dispatch technicians daily across the city's neighborhoods and six nearby towns, all reached on main roads.

Home Zone

Loveland, CO 80537 And 80538

Loveland is our home base, and our office on Main Street is where every daily route starts. The city is not one uniform market. It is a set of neighborhoods with different housing ages, different lot sizes, and different plumbing, and we plan each call around which one you live in.

Historic Downtown And West Central

The oldest housing, near the Rialto Theater and Loveland Museum. Older supply and drain lines are the main concern.

North Lake And Lake Loveland

Older single-family streets with mature trees, close to North Lake Park and the Benson Sculpture Garden.

The Boyd Lake Area

Newer homes around Boyd Lake State Park, many on larger irrigated lots.

Mariana Butte

A foothills golf-course community west of downtown, with homes from the 1990s through today.

Centerra And The Lakes At Centerra

A 3,000-acre master-planned community at I-25 and US-34, anchored by the Promenade Shops and Chapungu Sculpture Park.

Big Thompson River Corridor

Homes near the river, where the September 2013 flood is still a reference point for drainage and sump pumps.

These neighborhoods are inside Loveland, so we cover them on this page and across our service pages rather than on separate location pages. The towns below are different. Each is its own market outside Loveland, with its own town government, housing history, and permit rules.

How We Drew The Map

Real Routes, Not Random City Names

We built this list around the roads our trucks actually drive. US-287 runs south to Berthoud, US-34 and I-25 reach Johnstown, Highway 60 continues east to Milliken, I-25 north serves Windsor and Timnath, and I-25 south reaches Mead. Every town here is roughly 9 to 16 miles from our office and sits on a main road, which keeps emergency response times realistic.

County lines matter too. Loveland and Timnath are in Larimer County. Berthoud, Johnstown, and Windsor straddle the Larimer and Weld county line, and Mead and Milliken sit in Weld County. Permits and inspections follow the local jurisdiction and the version of the International Plumbing Code (IPC) it has adopted, and our technicians handle those differences on permitted work.

Beyond The Map

Fort Collins, Longmont, Greeley, And Estes Park

Fort Collins, Longmont, Greeley, and Estes Park are large neighboring markets outside our regular routes. We do not run daily dispatch there, so call and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you the same day. The same goes for rural properties west of Loveland, around Masonville and the Carter Lake area.

If your address is not listed above but sits near one of these towns, call anyway. We route by real driving distance from Loveland, not strict town boundaries, so a property just outside a town line is often still within easy reach. Call (970) 708-6013 with your address and we will confirm.

Why These Six And Not Others

Closer Towns First, Then Outward

A good service area grows outward from the home base in the order a real dispatcher would choose. Berthoud and Johnstown are the closest towns and sit on straight roads from Loveland, so they come first. Windsor, Timnath, Mead, and Milliken follow, each about 14 to 16 miles out and reached on I-25 or Highway 60 rather than through city traffic.

We skipped towns that look close on a map but are awkward to reach, and we skipped larger cities that already have plenty of local plumbers. That is why the list stops at six towns instead of stretching to every name within thirty miles. A shorter, honest list means a technician who actually arrives when we say.

What Changes By Town

Old Town Cores And New Subdivisions

Every town on this page has the same two-part pattern. A small old core with early-1900s housing sits in the middle, and newer subdivisions surround it. The old cores bring aging supply lines and sewer laterals, while the subdivisions bring water heater sizing, fixture warranty issues, and hard water scale. Each town page explains which pattern dominates locally.
