Local Plumbing Challenges

Why LaPorte Homes Call Us

Older Housing Stock

First settled in 1844, LaPorte has some of the oldest homes in the area, more prone to aging pipe material than the newer towns nearby.

Foothills, Rural Character

A rural, foothills community rather than a dense subdivision, with more well and septic considerations on some properties.

River-Adjacent Properties

Homes along the Cache la Poudre River corridor sometimes deal with higher groundwater and drainage considerations.

Slower, Steadier Growth

Unlike the boomtowns nearby, LaPorte's population has stayed relatively stable rather than exploding with new construction.

LaPorte's history runs deeper than any other town in this network, first settled in 1844 by Kit Carson's party of trappers along the Cache la Poudre River, and later recognized as the first white community established in what's now Larimer County. That history matters for plumbing in a very practical way. A rural community that's grown slowly rather than exploded into new subdivisions has a much higher share of homes old enough to still carry original galvanized supply lines or aging cast iron drains. It's the same real risk factor we watch for in Berthoud's historic downtown, just across a larger share of the town rather than one older pocket of it. LaPorte is also governed directly by Larimer County rather than having its own town government, which doesn't change how we dispatch or price a call, but is worth knowing if a permit ever needs to be pulled for larger repiping or sewer work.

LaPorte falls under Poudre School District R-1, the same district that covers Fort Collins, rather than the Thompson district that covers Loveland and Berthoud. That's a reminder that LaPorte's day-to-day orientation has always run more toward Fort Collins than toward the towns to the south and east. Median household income here runs noticeably below the boomtowns nearby, which tracks with the older, more modest housing stock that makes up much of the town.

The foothills setting brings its own real consideration too. Some LaPorte properties sit on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal water and sewer, which is a genuinely different service scope from anything in Loveland's newer growth suburbs. It's worth mentioning when you call so a technician arrives prepared for whichever system a specific property actually has.
