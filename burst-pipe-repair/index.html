Signs Of A Burst Pipe

What To Watch For

Sudden Water Flow

Water appearing where there's no obvious fixture nearby.

Hissing Or Dripping In Walls

A sound of running water with nothing turned on.

Spreading Water Stains

Discoloration on a ceiling or wall that keeps growing.

Sudden Pressure Drop

A pipe failing elsewhere in the system can drop pressure at every fixture.

Right Now: What To Do Before We Arrive

  • Shut off the main water valve immediately. This stops the flow at the source rather than just the one fixture.
  • Turn off electricity to any affected area if water is anywhere near outlets, switches, or a panel.
  • Move furniture, rugs, and electronics away from the water if it's safe to do so.
  • Call our emergency line. Don't wait to see if it stops on its own.
Why Loveland Sees This

Freeze-Thaw Is The Main Cause Here

Winter freeze-thaw cycles along the Front Range are the leading cause of burst pipes in this area, especially in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages that don't get much attention until a cold snap. Water expands as it freezes inside a pipe, and that expansion is what splits the pipe wall, usually revealing itself only once things thaw and the water starts flowing. Older homes with pipe runs added before modern insulation codes are the most exposed.

High water pressure is a less obvious but real contributor too. A home running well above the typical residential range puts constant stress on aging fittings and solder joints, and a pipe that's already thin from corrosion is more likely to fail under that extra pressure during a freeze event than a healthy one would be. Checking pressure isn't usually the first thing a homeowner thinks to do after a burst pipe, but it's worth asking about if this isn't the first time it's happened in the same house.

How It Gets Fixed

Repair Vs. Reroute

A single, cleanly split section of pipe is usually cut out and replaced with new pipe and fittings in one visit. A pipe run that's frozen and burst more than once, or one sitting in a chronically cold, exposed location, sometimes gets rerouted through a warmer path or wrapped with insulation as part of the fix, rather than patched in the same vulnerable spot again. Any replacement section still has to meet the same pipe material and support standards set out in the plumbing code Colorado has adopted, patch or full reroute.

Where Pipes Freeze

Cold-Snap Calls From Downtown To Mariana Butte

Freeze calls cluster in the same places, garages, crawl spaces, and exterior walls on the north side of a house. Older bungalows in West Central Loveland often have exposed runs in crawl spaces, while hillside homes in Mariana Butte can have outdoor spigots and lines that take the brunt of winter wind. Knowing where your main shutoff is before a cold snap is the single most useful thing you can do.

Valves And Fittings

A Broken Valve, A Cracked Elbow, Or A Pipe You Cannot Shut Off

Not every burst is a split pipe. A cracked elbow, a snapped supply valve behind a toilet, or a failed PVC connector can spray just as hard. The first question is always whether the water can be stopped.

When The Shutoff Will Not Close

If the fixture valve is the broken part, close the main. If the main will not close or leaks around its stem, say so on the call, since the plan changes. Leave other fixtures closed until the repair is done.

Copper, PVC, And CPVC Repairs

A small failure in copper can often be cut out and sleeved. Brittle plastic fittings usually need the section replaced, and old CPVC may show the same weakness in several places. If a second failure appears in the same area, the repiping conversation is worth having.

Crews And Exposed Lines

Sometimes a utility crew unearths a leak at the street or the meter and the owner needs a plumber to follow up. That is a service line question, covered on the water line page.
