About Loveland Pro Plumbing

Licensed plumbing for Loveland, CO and the surrounding Northern Colorado towns, with upfront pricing and a real person answering the phone 24/7.

Who We Are

Local Plumbing, Done Plainly

Loveland Pro Plumbing is based in Loveland, Colorado, at 968 Main St, Suite 19, and we dispatch technicians across Loveland and the nearby Larimer and Weld County towns of Berthoud, Windsor, Johnstown, Timnath, Mead, and Milliken. We handle everything from routine repairs to full emergency response, with the same flat-price-before-work approach on every call, day or night.

What We Actually Believe In

Upfront Pricing, No Surprises

Every job gets a flat price before any work begins, emergency or not. We don't add surprise charges once a technician is already on site, and we tell you plainly when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, or the other way around, rather than defaulting to whichever is more expensive.

Licensing

Licensed Under Colorado's DORA Framework

Our technicians are licensed in accordance with Colorado's DORA plumbing regulations, and every job we complete, from a same-day faucet repair to a full sewer line replacement, follows the same Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards the city inspects new construction against.

Why Front Range Water Comes Up So Often

Hard Water Shapes A Lot Of What We Do

Loveland and the surrounding towns draw from Front Range water with real mineral hardness, which is why so much of our work, from tankless water heater flushes to water softener installs, traces back to the same underlying cause. We built our service menu around what actually fails here, not a generic national template.

Where We Work

Loveland And The Surrounding Towns

Beyond Loveland itself, we regularly dispatch to Berthoud, Windsor, Johnstown, Timnath, Mead, and Milliken, each with its own real mix of older and newer housing stock that shapes what kind of call is most common there. See our full service area for details on each town.

What We Don't Do

A Few Honest Limitations

We don't offer financing programs, and we don't work outside our stated service area without confirming coverage first. If a job is outside our scope, like a full septic system installation, we'll tell you directly rather than taking on work outside what we're actually equipped for.

Get In Touch

Ready When You Are

Whether it's a routine repair or a 2am emergency, our line is answered 24/7. See our full list of services, or just call directly with what's going on.

How We Handle A Typical Call

From First Call To Fixed

When you call, we ask a few questions about what's happening, which usually gives us a good sense of what's likely going on before a technician even arrives. On site, the technician confirms the diagnosis, gives you a flat price, and completes the work in that same visit for the large majority of calls. We test everything before we leave, and we tell you honestly if something needs a follow-up visit rather than claiming a fix that isn't actually complete.

A Note On Emergencies

Real 24/7 Coverage

Our emergency line is genuinely answered around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays, not a call center that takes a message until business hours. For a burst pipe, no hot water, or a sewage backup, that distinction matters, and it's part of why we built our dispatch around actually being reachable rather than just advertising availability.

Working With Property Managers

Commercial And Multi-Unit Properties

Beyond individual homeowners, we also work directly with property managers on multi-unit buildings and with local businesses on scheduled commercial maintenance, from grease trap service to backflow testing. See our commercial plumbing page for more on how that works.

A Straightforward Way To Reach Us

Call, Don't Fill Out A Form

We don't use a contact form as the primary way to reach us, since a real conversation gets you a faster, more accurate read on your situation than a web form ever could. Call directly, day or night, and you'll talk to someone who can actually answer questions and get a technician moving if needed, rather than waiting for a callback from a submitted form.

Serving The Towns Around Loveland

Not Just One City

We built this business around actually knowing the towns we serve, not just adding a city name to a generic template. Each of the nearby towns we cover, Berthoud, Windsor, Johnstown, Timnath, Mead, and Milliken, has its own real housing history and water quality considerations that we take into account before a technician ever knocks on the door.

One Last Thing

We'd Rather Be Honest Than Sell You Something

If a repair is genuinely the better option over a replacement, we'll say so, even though a replacement is usually the larger job. That approach costs us some revenue in the short term, but it's the only way we're comfortable running this business long-term in a community where we expect to see the same customers again.

We're happy to talk through what's going on first.
